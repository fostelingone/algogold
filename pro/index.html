How It Works

AI Gold DCA Preset — XAU/USD trading system based on grid averaging

The preset works on a grid averaging strategy with dynamic risk management. This is not classic martingale — every position is calculated algorithmically, with a strict leverage cap and a built-in basket stop-out.

The system does three things that a manual trader physically cannot do at the same time:

01
Adaptive entry
The algorithm monitors XAU/USD volatility in real time and opens the first position only when conditions match historical reversal patterns. Not on a schedule — only when the market gives a setup.
02
Position size & grid logic
Each subsequent position in the grid is scaled to your balance and remaining leverage. Strict cap: the system cannot open more than N levels, regardless of price movement. This eliminates the classic martingale trap.
03
Exit through recovery zones
The basket closes when price reaches the recovery zone — a calculated point where the combined position turns profitable. Average trade time: 1 hour 11 minutes. No positions stuck for days or weeks.
